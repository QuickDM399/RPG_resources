**What the script does now (v0.14.0).** Command `!bitdcrew <verb>`, variable `BitDCrewTAM`, state key `BitDCrewTAM`, ability marker `bitd-crew-tam`, global macro `CREW_TAM`. Crew sheets only (`sheet_type` crew); GM any crew, players only crews they control. `~ Rebuild` rebuilds the token actions (needed after a module or cohort change). Token actions, in bar order: `1. Roll` (prompts: Tier, Entanglement for core crews, one per cohort, bonus dice), `2. Engagement` (composed roll with the book's Major Advantage questions, ticked abilities and claims added by plan type, buttons for Predators and party abilities, outcome named), `3. Fortune`, `4. Score` (core: Heat only; Deep Cuts Downtime crews: Heat, Rep, Fallout buttons for claims, Payoff walk-through, tithe, seized assets, Misdirection, Edge for the party), `5. Downtime` (Deep Cuts Downtime crews only: the Heat and Hold hub), `6. Abilities`, `6a. Contacts`, `6b. Claims`, `6c. Crew Upgrades`, `6d. Cohorts`, `7. Adjust`, `8. Clocks`, `9. Status`, `~ Rebuild`. Automatic: Heat reaching 9 adds a Wanted level and rolls over; token bar 1 is linked to Heat.

- **Claims (6b), upgrades (6c):** a card per claim or upgrade with the rule in force, the held or taken state, and the other text behind a button; text is verbatim from the books, with the Deep Cuts replacements when the module is on; styled HTML cards when `!bitdcrew fmt card html`.
- **Cohorts (6d), v0.12.x:** list card (whisper), public status card per cohort (condition, harm circles, types and edges and flaws with book text, quality and scale, armor, notes), roll buttons -3d to +3d, Hooked (+1 quality for a gang with a savage, unreliable or wild flaw, max 4). **Harm is additive** (user ruling: level 1 + level 2 = Broken, 1 + 3 = Destroyed; boxes fill up to the level; Destroyed is all three boxes plus a flag in state); armor (Vehicle upgrade permanent; Leader and Reavers granted per fight by a button; a cohort with no armor keeps its armor box marked); heal one or two levels; replace a Destroyed cohort for Tier +2 Coin; "heal every cohort one level".
- **Cohort editor (v0.13.x):** a whisper card, one click per change: cohort type (Gang, Elite, Expert), the five gang types (toggles, for experts too), edges and flaws (vehicle tables for vehicle cohorts), and a "Write in a type" button that uses a **Roll20 query in a chat button (this works; the user confirmed the prompt opens)**. Soft warnings only (one or two edges and an equal number of flaws; up to two types); costs are shown and never spent. Boxes are rewritten in book spelling; made-up names kept with a Remove button.
- **Heat and Hold (v0.14.0):** buttons on the hub card, all logged in the Downtime summary: heal every cohort one level (once), Reduce Heat for 1 Coin or Rep, **Status** (reads the faction sheet: a character with `sheet_type` faction, rows `repeating_factions1` to `8` with name, tier as a Roman numeral, status; each Tier III or higher faction at Status +2 or more gives Heat -1, once per Downtime, never below 0; one faction sheet is used automatically, several are listed and the pick is remembered per crew, none gives hand-set Heat -1, -2, -3), GM ruling Heat -1, -2, -3 (any number), Just Passing Through, income claims, Assess hold, End Downtime (No Traces). The faction sheet is only read.
- **Tools:** `!bitdcrew debug on|off` (GM, writes steps to the API console), `fmt` (card formatting and `fmt card html|sheet`), `party` (lists Roll20 Party members; Leader and Edge read them).
