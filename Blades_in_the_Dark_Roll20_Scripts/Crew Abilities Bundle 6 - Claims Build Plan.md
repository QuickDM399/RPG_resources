## 2. What I would build

### A. Heat per score (automatic)

- Cover Operation, Bluecoat Intimidation and Bluecoat Confidants each take 2 off the Fallout Heat total. A line on the card names each, for example "Cover Operation: -2 Heat". The Heat never goes below 0.
- This applies to the core Score card as well (the rule is identical), not only Deep Cuts.
- The reduction applies to the Fallout total only. Heat from fencing seized assets is added later and is not reduced.
- Rep follows the reduced Heat, the same way No Traces already works (Rep is "1 Rep for every 2 Heat generated by the score", Deep Cuts p81).

### B. Rep per score

- **Victim Trophies** (+1 Rep per score) is automatic, counted in the same Rep gain as the Score. One line on the card. Leverage's +1 applies once to the whole gain, as today.
- **Publicity** and **Doskvol's Most Wanted** depend on the kind of score, which the script cannot see. When ticked, the Fallout card gets a button for each ("This was a takedown: +2 Rep"). Once per Score. Misdirection's offer counts only the Rep the Score gave automatically (inference: the buttons come after the offer).

### C. Payoff coin (+2) as buttons on the Fallout card

- Envoy, Fixer, Local Graft, Loyal Fence and Surplus Caches, when ticked, each add a button such as "Envoy: high-class clients, +2 Coin". Once per Score each.
- The coin is added to the Payoff before the seized-assets step, so it counts toward the tithe ("+2 coin in payoff"). After the seized step is chosen the buttons say it is too late.

### D. Claim income in the Heat and Hold card

- For each ticked income claim the card gets a button, once per Downtime per claim (the existing Downtime ledger holds the record).
- The click rolls dice equal to the crew's Tier, takes the highest, subtracts the Heat at that moment (not below 0), and whispers the result with an **Add N Coin** button. Coin is applied only by that button, up to the crew's vault room, as with the deposit. The rest is "record by hand".
- A Tier 0 crew rolls 2d and keeps the lowest, as every zero-dice roll does (inference: the book does not say).

### E. Status

`8. Status` gets one line listing the claims in play and what they do, for example "Claims in play: Cover Operation (-2 Heat per score), Vice Den (income)". After the Misdirection lesson, nothing here should fail silently.
